Choosing a B2B lead generation agency starts with the work you need someone else to own. A sales team with a usable account list may need consistent calling and follow-up. A company entering several countries may need regional SDR teams, while another needs the data and systems to support its own representatives.

This guide covers Belkins, Callbox, CIENCE, memoryBlue, SalesHive and CallTeam. The other providers appear alphabetically, followed by a separate explanation of CallTeam's service, evidence and buying fit. For a broader procurement checklist, use our [guide to choosing a lead generation service](/articles/b2b-lead-generation-services/).

## Research date and comparison method

Provider information was last verified on October 26, 2026 against the official pages linked below. CallTeam publishes this commercial comparison and sells one of the services discussed. We reviewed public descriptions and offers; we have not run or independently measured the competitors' campaigns.

The assessment considers the operating model, channels, assigned people, pricing structure, qualification and sales handoff. Buyer-fit comments are our judgement based on those published descriptions. A feature missing from a reviewed page is something to confirm, rather than evidence that a provider cannot supply it.

Dollar figures reproduce the currency notation displayed by each provider. Where a page leaves currency, billing period or minimum term unclear, obtain those details in the proposal. Performance targets and guarantees also require their own written definitions.

## What each model means for the buyer

### Belkins: specify the package and channel allocation

Belkins' [pricing comparison](https://belkins.io/pricing) distinguishes Growth, Growth Plus and a small-business option delivered through partner agencies. Calling coverage differs between those offers. A buyer should therefore identify the actual delivery package instead of assuming that every Belkins engagement contains the same mix of outreach.

Ask for the people, channels and meeting definition attached to the proposal. The most useful comparison is the work purchased for your market, including who handles responses and how unsuccessful bookings are treated.

### Callbox: examine the regional operating scope

Callbox's appointment-setting service describes a managed team, multichannel engagement and international coverage. It may merit a closer look when the brief includes several territories or a broader demand-generation program.

Bring the regional plan into the buying discussion. Confirm the assigned languages, time zones, account groups and handoff process, then identify which services your internal marketing or sales team will continue to provide.

### CIENCE: add the components before judging the price

CIENCE separates its campaign team, platform and SDR charges. Its current pricing page also describes a one-time setup stage and performance bonuses, so the recurring headline components are only part of the commercial review.

The setup section describes the preparation but does not state an amount. Request the setup quote and bonus calculation, then compare equivalent staffing and responsibilities over your intended engagement period.

### memoryBlue: confirm the current business and delivery team

memoryBlue offers outsourced sales development alongside other services for technology companies. It [acquired Operatix in 2023](https://memoryblue.com/blog/memoryblue-acquires-operatix-to-create-a-global-leader-in-sales-acceleration-services-for-the-b2b-technology-ecosystem/), so an old Operatix shortlist should be checked against the current organization.

For an international technology campaign, ask which team will work your accounts and which activities sit inside the quote. Recruitment, training and outsourced execution address different staffing problems even when the same provider can discuss all three.

### SalesHive: compare the specific SDR plan

SalesHive's current offer includes a phone-only Starter plan as well as plans combining calls and email. Its pricing page also distinguishes US-based and offshore SDR options, with a custom rate for the selected coverage.

Ask how the chosen plan allocates people and activity to your account. Team continuity, coaching, reporting and the agreed definition of a useful meeting deserve attention alongside the channel list.

## When CallTeam is a practical fit

Consider CallTeam when your main gap is the day-to-day work of researching suitable accounts, reaching buyers, following up and arranging a qualified conversation for your sales team. A defined B2B offer, meaningful customer value and sellers ready to act on meetings create a sensible starting point.

Our [outsourced SDR service](/services/outsourced-sdr/) connects targeting, scripts, human calling, agreed follow-up, qualification, scheduling, CRM notes and reporting. AI-assisted research supports preparation; the person speaking with the prospect is responsible for testing the assumptions and recording what the buyer actually says.

The campaign-ready program [starts at US$4,000 per month](/pricing/). It assumes an established offer, defined market, target accounts or contacts ready to activate, CRM and calendar access, and a sales team able to handle meetings. Fresh prospect research, extra markets and expanded capacity are scoped before launch; most programs begin with a three-month engagement.

The fit conversation should establish what CallTeam will own, what you will supply and how you will assess progress. If you require a particular caller location, language or data-ownership term, have it confirmed for the proposed engagement.

## Define the handoff your sellers need

Before appointing an agency, write down the information that should accompany a meeting. Include the account fit, buyer's role, reason for discussing the offer, timing, agreed purpose, outstanding questions and next owner. Our [appointment-setting service](/services/b2b-appointment-setting/) explains how those responsibilities connect to booking and follow-up.

An illustrative handoff might say that an operations director wants to examine a costly reconciliation workflow, while finance approval and the implementation date remain unconfirmed. Those unknowns give sales something useful to investigate. A calendar invitation saying only that the prospect is interested leaves the seller to reconstruct the conversation.

Agree how sales will report acceptance, attendance and the next commercial step. The [outbound appointment-setting guide](/articles/outbound-appointment-setting/) develops that quality standard, while our [AI lead generation explanation](/articles/ai-lead-generation/) describes the supporting research layer.

## Put comparable costs beside comparable responsibilities

Use the same currency, evaluation period, data requirements and staffing assumptions for each proposal. Separate one-time setup charges, recurring service fees, software, variable payments and work your own team must perform.

For example, 13 four-week billing periods cover 52 weeks, while a calendar-month agreement normally has 12 monthly charges in a year. That arithmetic matters for a long-running engagement, although the actual contract determines what you pay and for how long.
