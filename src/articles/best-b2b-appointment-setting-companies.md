An appointment-setting company should make the handoff from prospecting to sales easier to trust. The commercial question is what the buyer agreed to discuss, whether the company and contact fit your market, and what the salesperson knows before the meeting starts.

This guide covers Belkins, Callbox, Leadium, SalesHive, SalesRoads and CallTeam. It compares the other five providers using their published offers, then explains CallTeam's own scope, proof and fit. Start with the meeting your sales team needs and use that definition to compare the people, channels and costs behind it.

## Understand the service behind each appointment offer

### Belkins

Belkins' appointment-setting offer includes research, booking and no-show recovery. Its [package page](https://belkins.io/pricing) distinguishes the available channel coverage, with a partner-delivered small-business option as well as Growth packages.

Ask which package is being proposed, who delivers it and how calling effort is allocated. A stated annual appointment target also needs definitions for attendance, rejection and any conditions attached to the commitment.

### Callbox

Callbox describes a dedicated appointment-setting team using multiple channels and international delivery. Its service information includes qualification and a handoff brief, making those processes appropriate subjects for a detailed proposal review.

For a regional or enterprise campaign, name the markets, buying roles and technical preparation required. Ask how the team records the reason for the meeting and handles a prospect who needs further nurturing.

### Leadium

Leadium publishes separate calling-only and multichannel monthly prices and describes month-to-month terms. Its company material positions the service as a US-based outbound team.

Confirm the current statement of work, setup charges, staffing and data coverage for your offer. Use the distinction between the two channel models to decide which activities you need transferred rather than assume that a higher fee always buys a better outcome.

### SalesHive

SalesHive offers phone-only as well as phone-and-email plans, with different SDR-location options. Its managed-service information also covers a strategist, data and supporting technology.

Ask which plan and team would represent you, how qualification is agreed and how the results appear in your sales workflow. Those details help compare its specific proposal with a calling-led CallTeam engagement.

### SalesRoads

The current SalesRoads pricing page shows $11,950 for one SDR per four-week engagement, supported by strategy, coaching, data and systems. Its [appointment-setting page](https://salesroads.com/appointment-setting-services/) still displays $9,500 per four weeks for Full SDR Appointment Setting.

Request a current written price and scope to resolve the difference. Also confirm the meeting definition, team allocation and the period over which your company will assess the engagement.

## Define a qualified appointment with your salespeople

A workable standard should cover account fit, the person's role, a relevant business issue and informed agreement to the meeting purpose. It should also identify what remains unknown so a first sales conversation is not represented as a fully qualified deal.

Write down exclusions such as existing opportunities, duplicates or companies outside your target market. Agree how a seller can query a booking and what evidence both teams will use to resolve it. The [appointment-setting buying guide](/articles/outsourced-b2b-appointment-setting/) can help establish that responsibility before you compare offers.

Keep booked meetings, attendance and accepted opportunities visible as separate stages. A useful appointment can still fail to become a customer, while a calendar booking can lack the evidence sales needs to prepare at all.

## Compare remedies, meeting costs and internal work

A meeting commitment needs a precise remedy. Continuing work, replacing a representative, replacing an appointment and refunding a fee each protect something different. Read the relevant conditions with the qualification standard instead of treating every advertised guarantee as equivalent.

For a simple cost illustration, a $6,000 program producing ten held meetings costs $600 per held meeting. If only six meet your agreed sales standard, the same fee represents $1,000 per held qualified meeting. Those are arithmetic examples, not provider benchmarks or forecasts.

Include your sellers' preparation, follow-up and management time when judging the overall investment.
