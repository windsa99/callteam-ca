## Which agency fits the work you need?

### Atlantic Growth Solutions

Choose AGS when your sales team needs a repeatable way to choose accounts and qualify leads. Its published lead generation service sets up the process. Plan to staff the calls and callbacks yourself if you buy that scope.

### DMT Business Development

Choose DMT when you need research, email and LinkedIn together. Starter leaves out cold calling. Standard lists calling as one option for the extra channel. If the phone is your main channel, get the calling scope in writing before you buy.

### Lead Generators International

Consider LGI if you want telephone appointment setting with an agreed minimum. Its published remedy is more work at its expense, subject to contract terms. If attendance drives your budget, check whether cancellations and no-shows count toward the minimum.

### Purple Sales

Consider Purple Sales when you need English and French outreach in the same program. Its appointment-setting offer uses custom plans. For a phone-only campaign, ask for a separate calling price so you can see what you would pay for the work you need.

### The Sales Factory

Shortlist The Sales Factory when you need managed sales development reps to test a market. Its public offer leaves rep allocation and rates to the quote. Get the weekly hours and who manages the rep before you pay for added capacity.

## What does a Canadian lead generation campaign cost?

Ask for setup, monthly fees, licensed data and meeting charges in the same currency. Get the minimum term and cancellation notice in writing.

An illustrative US$12,000 campaign with 20 bookings costs US$600 per booking. If 12 meetings take place, each held meeting costs US$1,000. If eight of those qualify, you paid US$1,500 per held, qualified meeting.

## How should an agency cover Canada and Quebec?

Give the agency the provinces you can serve and the industries you want to reach. For a Canada-to-US campaign, request separate account lists and reporting. The seller reviewing the results should be able to see which market produced a meeting and what the caller learned there.

If French is required, ask the provider to walk you through one buyer conversation in French. Include the opener, an objection, written follow-up and the CRM notes. If your seller will take the meeting in French, agree who owns that step before the first call.

## What should sales get before each meeting?

Ask each finalist for a sample meeting record. Check that it names the attendees, records what the caller promised and flags what sales still needs to confirm.

Say an Ontario manufacturer wants to discuss supplier delays and asks to bring in its purchasing director. The handoff should name the contact who raised the issue, the agreed next step and who still needs to join. Use the free [Meeting Quality Check](/b2b-meeting-quality-check/) to track which booked meetings took place and were accepted by sales.

## What calling rules should agencies address?

The [CRTC’s business calling guidance](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/phone/telemarketing/biz.htm) explains the National Do Not Call List exemption and the rules that still apply. Ask who manages registration, caller identification and internal do-not-call requests. For commercial email or texts, ask how the provider follows the [CRTC’s CASL guidance](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/internet/anti/reg.htm).

## How to request a quote from your shortlist

Send the same brief to the two providers you would actually hire. Include the companies and job roles you want, the provinces and languages, what counts as a meeting, and which seller will take it. Specify who should report results and how often.

Ask what happens to an interested buyer who needs to wait until next quarter. A useful plan names the person who owns the callback, where the date is recorded and whether follow-up continues if your contract ends. Also ask who updates the CRM if the buyer moves companies during the campaign. These details affect how much work your sellers inherit.

If you already have a list, ask what the agency will check before using it. Have the provider explain how existing customers, active deals and stop requests will be excluded. Then ask to see the first progress report your team would receive and which numbers you can export.
