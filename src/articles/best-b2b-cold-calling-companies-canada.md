## Understand what the other companies provide

### Cold Calling Co.: a project-based calling option

Cold Calling Co. describes verified or cleaned prospect lists, business-specific scripts and weekly reporting, with a project-based commercial model. Its service page also states that the client keeps the list built or verified for the campaign.

That can be relevant for an established B2B business with a bounded calling project. Ask how the project defines completion, handles callbacks and transfers interested prospects to your sellers after the initial call.

### Lead Generators International: appointments and research

LGI presents telephone appointment setting and market research among its services. Its [results guarantee](https://clientfinders.com/what-we-do/results-guarantee/) describes continuing campaign work at its own expense until an agreed minimum is reached, subject to the agreement's conditions.

Read that wording with the meeting definition, exclusions and remedy. Ask how a disputed appointment is reviewed and whether the proposed output concerns meetings, demos or another deliverable.

### Purple Sales: calling with wider channel support

Purple Sales' calling information describes English-French capability and coordinated sales and marketing services. A buyer needing bilingual outreach should confirm the people assigned and the follow-up they can support.

Its [appointment-setting page](https://mypurplesales.com/b2b-appointment-setting-companies/) describes custom planning. Establish how the quote divides effort between calling and other work so your comparison reflects the campaign you will receive.

### The Sales Factory: sales development and research support

The Sales Factory presents a data-led sales and lead-generation model with onshore talent. Its broader service description covers market discovery, launch, growth and expansion, giving buyers several possible ways to engage.

Ask how the proposed SDR is managed, how account research is supplied and what your internal team must do. The exact assignment should make the distinction between added capacity and a managed result clear.

## Define the Canadian calling job

Start with the audience and the reason for a conversation. Give the provider your industries, company criteria, provinces, buyer roles and exclusions, then explain what problem the offer helps solve. A useful script depends on that commercial context.

Decide who will supply contact data and what happens when the list needs correction. Also agree the calling windows, repeat attempts, callbacks and follow-up ownership. The [cold-call script library](/resources/cold-call-scripts/) can help you examine how a relevant opening connects to discovery and a reasonable next step.

Where French is required, ask about conversation quality, written follow-up and the receiving salesperson's language. Confirm caller location separately from language and Canadian market coverage, since those requirements do not automatically mean the same thing.

## Check Canadian calling requirements

The CRTC says calls to business numbers are exempt from the National DNCL rules while remaining covered by Telemarketing and ADAD rules. Review its [exemption explanation](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/phone/telemarketing/exempt.htm) and [telemarketing requirements](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/phone/telemarketing/tobligations/rules-regles.htm) when preparing the operating discussion.

Ask who handles caller identification, requests to stop contact and the requirements relevant to the target numbers and calling method. If the campaign also uses email or text, review those channels separately with appropriate advice for the work involved.

For a wider mix of marketing and sales services, the [Canadian lead generation company comparison](/articles/best-b2b-lead-generation-companies-canada/) addresses a broader purchase. This page focuses on deciding who should perform and manage the phone work.

## Request a complete price for the work

Ask for the list source, research, call resources, follow-up, CRM work and management to be itemized in the proposal. Confirm the initial duration and any setup or optional charges alongside the recurring or project fee.

Use the same campaign assumptions across finalists. A quote that expects you to supply cleaned contacts and manage interested replies transfers different work from a program that includes those tasks.
