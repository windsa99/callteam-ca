CallTeam has received recognition in the **Global 100, 2027 edition**, in the category **B2B Lead Generation & Appointment Setting - Canada**. The Global Publishing Media Group judging panel highlighted CallTeam’s managed outbound services, campaign examples and free sales resources.

The feedback connects our managed outbound service with the practical sales knowledge we share publicly. Together, these help businesses plan better outreach and put experienced people behind the execution.

## Global 100 recognition for B2B lead generation

The judges described CallTeam as a Canadian specialist that “pairs hands-on outbound execution with unusually open, practical education”. Their comments highlighted prospect research, human calling, follow-up, qualification and appointment setting, including handoffs with buyer context, objections and next steps.

CallTeam is also an International Elite 100 Global Awards 2026 winner for outbound calling and appointment setting. Explore both honours on our [awards and recognition page](/awards/).

## Campaign examples referenced by the judges

The panel referred to CallTeam’s published campaign examples when explaining its decision. These included [11 qualified demos during a 90-day SaaS campaign](/case-studies/saas-demo-appointment-setting/) and [nine qualified appointments in the first 90 days of a manufacturing cybersecurity campaign](/case-studies/manufacturing-cybersecurity-appointment-setting/).

These figures describe those specific engagements. The case studies explain the buyer context, outreach approach and subsequent outcomes so a prospective client can assess the work in more detail.

For our B2B lead generation and appointment booking service, the meeting is one stage in a larger process. We prepare the target accounts, speak with relevant people, follow up and transfer what the buyer has actually said to the client’s sales team.

## Making outbound sales knowledge accessible

CallTeam publishes 100 free cold-call scripts and 138 outbound sales guides. The [outbound sales library](/resources/outbound-sales-library/) organises material around scripts, industries, buyers, objections and campaign strategy, helping sellers find guidance relevant to the task in front of them.

The library also connects with interactive planning tools. Founders can work through buyer language and campaign questions before deciding whether to manage the outreach themselves or engage a service provider.

That combination of education and execution was central to the judges’ feedback. It reflects how we support businesses at different stages, including people who use the free material without becoming clients.

## Put the recognition into a campaign discussion

Start with the market you need to reach, the problem your offer addresses and the evidence a buyer would need before agreeing to meet. Use those inputs to discuss targeting, outreach language and what should count as a qualified appointment.

The campaign preview helps organise that preparation by selecting relevant openings, questions and guidance. It gives you concrete material to review before a conversation about a managed campaign.
