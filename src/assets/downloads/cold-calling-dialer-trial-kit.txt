QUESTIONS TO ASK SALES

1. Quote the exact dialer-enabled plan, currency, minimum paid seats, billing term and payment schedule. What renews automatically, and when must we cancel?

2. Which features are included: power, parallel, predictive, human detection, coaching, recording and callbacks? Which require an add-on or different tier?

3. What costs extra for numbers, destinations, minutes, credits, voicemail connections and inbound calls? What is the rounding rule? What does “unlimited” exclude?

4. What happens when two people answer at once, a screening assistant answers, or no rep is free? Show us the call log and retry behaviour.

5. Show our exact CRM fields, dispositions, duplicate handling and callback tasks. What requires another connector or subscription?

6. Can we reduce seats mid-term? What happens to our numbers, recordings, notes and unused credits when we leave? Put the answers in the offer.
