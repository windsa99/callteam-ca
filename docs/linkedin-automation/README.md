## Editorial direction

Professional, blunt opinion tied to a specific price or workflow. Question unnecessary upgrades, seat minimums, team-wide add-ons and unused AI or personalisation features, including for large companies. State when a cheaper tier covers the job. Distinguish useful vendor controls from an attractive plan name.

No invented product tests, internal CallTeam software disclosures, unsupported channel rankings or promised conversion uplift. The short LinkedIn platform-rule note is relevant to this purchase. Material pricing conflicts are stated once in the relevant review. Gong's figures are identified as email reply rates; the reader is shown how to measure calls alongside LinkedIn.

No repeated long paragraphs, defensive benchmark disclaimers, “why this matters” filler or “X, not Y” framing. Parallel comparison labels are retained for scanning.
