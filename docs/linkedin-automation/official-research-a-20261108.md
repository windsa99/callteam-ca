# LinkedIn automation research — 8 November 2026

Scope: official vendor pricing, help articles and terms for Dripify, Expandi, HeyReach and Waalaxy. Documentation research only; no product trials, account changes or purchases. Exact source links and short excerpts appear below. Prices remain in the vendor's published currency; no CAD conversion. Calculated totals exclude taxes, LinkedIn subscriptions, email mailboxes/domains and optional services.

## Dripify

**Assessment:** A sensible choice for a small sales team wanting LinkedIn and email sequences in the same product. Pro supplies the practical campaign/inbox/export baseline. Advanced adds team management, tagging, A/B tests and step analytics. The cost rises with active accounts, and the included email-finding allowance is modest. Use Advanced in the comparison table so the team controls match the advertised configuration.

**D1 — Current pricing and product scope**  
https://dripify.com/pricing/  
Excerpt: “Each Dripify seat connects one LinkedIn account.”

- Live page has Pro US$79 monthly / US$59 monthly equivalent annually; Advanced US$99 / US$79; Enterprise custom. The page currently omits Basic; cached search results still show Basic US$59/39. Treat Basic as unresolved legacy pricing, not the current entry plan.
- Both published paid tiers include unlimited LinkedIn/email sequences, dedicated inbox and integrations. Advanced adds team management and campaign-analysis features. CSV export is in the comparison matrix and cached Pro description; see D4 for export documentation.
- Every paid plan includes 100 successful email-find/verify credits monthly, expiring after 30 days. Extra packs: US$29/1,000, $49/2,000, $69/5,000, $99/10,000. Sending is not credit-metered.
- Sales Navigator is optional. Cloud campaigns run without the user's browser or computer staying on. Ten-plus seats and invoice consolidation are referred to sales; no numerical small-team discount is published.

**D2 — Team licensing and duplicate filtering**  
https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/10259944-managing-multiple-teams-in-dripify  
Excerpt: “the team owner needs to be on the Advanced plan”

The June 2026 help article says members can use their own plans; owner must have Advanced. Roles are Owner/Manager/User. Team dashboard, shared templates and team-level lead filtering are documented. Filtering prevents adding leads already used in teammates' campaigns. A lower mixed configuration of one Advanced owner plus Pro senders is therefore supported: 1/3/5 senders would be US$99/$257/$415 monthly, or $79/$197/$315 monthly equivalent on annual billing. Label that exact mix if using it. If the owner does not send, ask whether their separate paid seat is necessary; the reviewed article does not settle a free non-sending manager option.

**D3 — Reply stopping**  
https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5914465-how-to-resume-a-sequence-for-a-lead-that-replied-to-your-message  
Excerpt: “pause the automated sequence once a response is detected”

Incoming replies pause a lead's sequence. A person can resume it from the inbox or lead profile. Sending a manual message through Dripify to a lead currently in a campaign also pauses their sequence.

**D4 — CSV workflow**  
https://help.dripify.com/en/articles/5612509-how-to-add-leads-from-sales-navigator-if-you-have-a-free-linkedin-account  
Excerpt: “click Export to download these leads into a CSV file”

Official help documents exporting imported campaign leads and uploading that CSV into another sender's campaign. This is usable data portability rather than a claim of a full CRM replacement.

**D5 — Currency, cancellation and refunds**  
https://dripify.com/terms-and-conditions/  
Excerpts: “All payments are in US Dollars.” / “All monthly purchases are non-refundable.”

Subscriptions auto-renew. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid term. An unused annual subscription is eligible for a refund within 24 hours after payment. Additional services are non-refundable. No card is needed for the seven-day trial; the account becomes restricted if no subscription is purchased.

## Expandi

**Assessment:** A strong fit when conditional sequences, several campaign types and LinkedIn/email steps matter. The documented workspace, inbox and export functions support operational use. At one to five senders its published monthly cost is higher than HeyReach Growth. Image/GIF/video personalization adds subscriptions. Avoid presenting every Agency control as guaranteed in Business because the official pricing and workspace pages disagree on the gate.

**E1 — Business and Agency plans**  
https://expandi.io/pricing/  
Excerpt: “Image & GIF Personalization This service requires an additional cost”

Business is $99/month; unlimited campaigns, conditional builder, LinkedIn/email sequences, analytics, tags/notes, duplication prevention and cloud operation. Image/GIF and Sendspark video personalization are paid extras. Agency is custom-priced for 10+ seats and lists flexible roles/permissions, centralized campaign management, client reports, template sharing and white label. No mandatory add-on for basic LinkedIn sequences is shown. Pricing page says both annual “20% Off” and “2 months free”; these do not produce the same annual total.

**E2 — Exact currency, unit and Sales Navigator**  
https://expandi.io/faq/  
Excerpt: “99 USD per account per month”

Sales Navigator is not required. Sales Navigator/Recruiter searches require the corresponding LinkedIn subscription. FAQ offers potential multi-account discounts by quote without a numerical rule. Its assertion of one plan with every feature conflicts with the current Business/Agency breakdown; do not repeat that blanket claim.

**E3 — Official annual rate and hosting**  
https://expandi.io/blog/b2b-prospecting-tool/  
Excerpt: “Business plan $99/month per seat, or $79/month billed annually.”

The vendor's own 2026 article confirms the $79 annual monthly equivalent and says cloud automation runs without the browser or computer on. $948/year is arithmetic from that rate, not a captured checkout total. Exclude the article's safety/performance rhetoric from CallTeam copy.

**E4 — Workspace users and permissions**  
https://expandi.io/workspaces/  
Excerpt: “Invite teammates or customers to your Workspace at no additional cost”

Documents predefined/custom roles, account sharing and permission settings without a visible Business paywall. The pricing page assigns flexible roles to Agency. Safest public wording: Business supports workspace collaboration; advanced Agency controls and white label need a quote. This page also contradicts itself on owner-only billing versus admin control; current help explicitly allows workspace owner/admin billing access.

**E5 — Seat billing**  
https://help.expandi.io/en/articles/10261458-manage-subscriptions-seats-for-your-linkedin-accounts-workspace  
Excerpt: “1 seat can be connected to (used for) one LinkedIn account at a time”

One subscription can hold several seats. Purchased seats are billed even when disconnected. Removing a LinkedIn account does not cancel the seat; reduce paid seat count. Additions prorate immediately, removals take effect next cycle. Seats can transfer within a workspace, not between workspaces. Each workspace has separate subscription management.

**E6 — Replies**  
https://help.expandi.io/en/articles/5405300-bulk-pause-and-continue  
Excerpt: “Once the lead replies to a message, the lead is automatically paused”

Manual replies/templates and bulk pause/resume are documented. Direct incoming messages and manual messages can also pause campaign leads.

**E7 — Duplicate handling**  
https://help.expandi.io/en/articles/14327909-manage-duplicates  
Excerpt: “decide where they should remain active”

Company-level and campaign-level duplication settings control overlap. Duplicate conflicts can stop progression; a report lets an operator keep a lead in one campaign and pause/remove other copies. Same-account outreach to the same lead in multiple simultaneous campaigns is not supported. Do not promise universal automatic cross-client deduplication.

**E8 — Inbox and export**  
https://help.expandi.io/en/articles/5405244-inbox  
Excerpt: “search for leads by name, job title, or company”

Inbox supports filters, tags, interaction status, manual replies and lead pause/resume. CSV export from contacts, lead lists and campaigns is separately documented at https://help.expandi.io/en/articles/5405850-how-to-export-data (excerpt: “export lists of leads as a CSV”).

**E9 — Cancellation/refund**  
https://expandi.io/terms-conditions/  
Excerpt: “subscription fees paid by the Customer are non-refundable”

Terms say a trial converts to paid unless canceled, subscriptions renew, cancellation normally takes effect at term end, and no cancellation refund is due except legal/contractual exceptions. This conflicts with the blog's no-card trial language; describe the offered seven-day trial, but do not promise no card/automatic charge without checking the actual sign-up offer.

## HeyReach

**Assessment:** The clearest fit here for several senders sharing one campaign and one inbox. Growth already includes the useful collaboration features; paying for teammates is unnecessary. Email sending requires a connected email outreach platform. The annual pricing display and help centre disagree, so use monthly prices for the main table. Permissions can be broader than a buyer expects.

**H1 — Live pricing**  
https://www.heyreach.io/pricing  
Excerpt: “100 enrichment credits per sender”

Growth: US$79/sender/month for 1–9; US$59 from ten, up to fifty. Agency: US$999/25 senders or $1,399/50. Unlimited: $2,999, with 300-sender fair-use cap. Done for you: custom. Teammates/clients are free.

Live annual Growth card: $64/month label, $759 billed yearly for one sender. Quarterly: $72/month label, $214 billed quarterly. The card's monthly equivalents are rounded; they do not multiply exactly to its period totals. Agency annual 25-sender card: $800/month, $9,591/year. Unlimited: $2,399/month, $28,790/year. Quote displayed totals without inventing exact multi-seat invoices.

Growth includes a proxy, unlimited campaigns, unified inbox, API/webhooks and permissions. Agency/Unlimited require own proxies or a custom arrangement. Included enrichment is a one-time allocation, not monthly. Email outreach uses Instantly/Smartlead integrations. Optional extra white-label brands: $500.

**H2 — Plan guide, currency and discrepancies**  
https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/14741630-heyreach-plans-and-pricing-what-s-included-and-how-to-choose  
Excerpt: “Pricing shown in this article is in USD, no VAT included.”

September 2026 help lists Growth $79/$71/$63 monthly/quarterly/yearly below ten, and $59/$53/$47 from ten. This conflicts with live rounded labels/totals; the article explicitly defers to the pricing page. It lists Agency 25 at $999/$899/$799 and Agency 50 at $1,399/$1,259/$1,119, also differing from live discounted labels. Annual is paid once per year. Added seats share the subscription's billing cycle; additions prorate, removals apply next renewal. Growth includes workspaces/permissions; Agency adds pooled client-workspace management/master view, branding and onboarding. The article's opening “no seat limits” description is qualified later by the 300 cap.

**H3 — Shared campaigns**  
https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/9897768-multiple-linkedin-senders-in-one-campaign-sender-rotation  
Excerpt: “This is a default feature.”

Two or more senders can share a campaign with automatic sender rotation. Limits are per sender across campaigns. InMail sequences require Sales Navigator on every selected sender. All senders' conversations can be managed together.

**H4 — Reply stopping**  
https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/9935919-why-are-my-leads-in-progress-not-enrolled-yet-finished-paused-pending-failed-excluded  
Excerpt: “Leads that respond to a sequence will end it automatically.”

**H5 — Duplicate exclusions**  
https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/9877952-how-to-launch-your-first-campaign-in-heyreach  
Excerpt: “Each lead will be checked”

Configurable exclusions cover leads contacted in other campaigns, messaged by other senders, contacted by the same sender, and blacklists. Scope is the same workspace; prior conversations must be recorded in HeyReach for the conversation filter. Eligibility is checked before each action. Do not claim organization-wide suppression across separate client workspaces.

https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/10493720-how-to-add-an-exclusion-list-to-my-campaign  
Excerpt: “cannot be used as an exclusion list”

Lists from CSV/API/Clay initially need LinkedIn enrichment/cross-checking before exclusion use. This matters when importing an external suppression list. Article documents an enrichment workaround and later additions to existing lists.

**H6 — Inbox and permission limits**  
https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/9877943-unibox-screen-overview  
Excerpt: “all LinkedIn senders, centralized in one place”

Team members can reply on a sender's behalf; tags, canned responses, filters and re-engagement are supported. User can track all recent LinkedIn conversations or only HeyReach-originated ones.

https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/11782284-how-to-invite-team-members-and-manage-role-based-permissions-in-heyreach  
Excerpt: “Permissions are set at the screen level”

Admin/member controls exist, but Inbox permission shows all conversations in a workspace, not just a member's own account. Screen-level permission is not item-level isolation.

**H7 — Exports and optional data costs**  
https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/10475396-leads-screen-overview  
Excerpt: “Export to CSV only provides HeyReach's default columns, not custom variables.”

Lead lists export to CSV; custom-variable export limitation should be stated if CSV portability is a buying criterion. Campaign status export is also documented at https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/15286042-how-to-see-who-accepted-your-connection-request-in-heyreach.

https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/12855506-how-do-the-email-credits-work-in-heyreach  
Excerpt: “Credits are never recurring and don’t expire.”

Extra enrichment bundles are separate purchases, organization-shared, and require an active plan/trial to use. One successful lookup uses one credit. Exact top-up prices are not published in the retrieved help text; do not invent them. These are optional for LinkedIn-only campaigns.

**H8 — LinkedIn dependency and hosted operation**  
https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/10415479-can-i-use-a-free-linkedin-account-with-heyreach  
Excerpt: “You can connect LinkedIn accounts without any subscription to HeyReach”

Free LinkedIn works; Sales Navigator is optional for ordinary LinkedIn outreach, required for documented InMail functionality. Connection guide https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/9877938-how-to-connect-my-linkedin-account-to-heyreach says cookie onboarding requires Chrome, while credentials are another option. That is an onboarding dependency, not evidence that campaigns require an open desktop browser. Terms H10 describe the service as hosted.

**H9 — Cancellation**  
https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/14723622-how-to-upgrade-downgrade-or-cancel-your-heyreach-subscription  
Excerpt: “Cancellation stops future renewals”

Help says cancel any time, continued access through the billing period, generally no refund after a period starts. Duplicate charges and billing errors are reviewed. It also says new senders/campaigns cannot be added once canceled. Do not promise unrestricted operations through the rest of a canceled term.

**H10 — Material terms conflict**  
https://www.heyreach.io/terms-of-service  
Excerpts: “an on-line, hosted solution” / “at least thirty (30) days prior”

Terms §7.1 default to written termination notice at least 30 days before expiry unless the Order Form states otherwise. This is materially stricter than H9's cancel-any-time description. Fees are generally non-refundable with specified contractual exceptions. Public copy should say cancellation stops renewal under the applicable subscription terms, or explicitly flag this contradiction. The terms also permit loss of data access 30 days after termination.

## Waalaxy

**Assessment:** Useful for teams happy with prebuilt sequences and predictable seat-based operation. Advanced is a sensible LinkedIn-only choice; Business adds native email. Inbox is extra, and a team cannot buy it for just one member. Annual discounts are substantial, but payment is upfront and cancellation does not shorten the commitment. Cloud operation changed in 2026, making many old reviews inaccurate.

**W1 — Current plans**  
https://www.waalaxy.com/pricing  
Excerpt: “Annual plans offer up to 50% savings with a single upfront payment.”

Displayed euro rates are Pro €19, Advanced €49, Business €69 per user/month on the annual-default presentation. Pro has 300 invitations/month; Advanced/Business 800 advertised. All show unlimited campaigns and prebuilt follow-ups. Business adds email/multichannel and 500 finder credits. Inbox is a paid add-on. CSV import/export and reply detection are listed. API/integration modules appear from Advanced. Enterprise is mentioned for 5+ users but lacks a separate public price. These advertised invitation allowances are product quotas, not guaranteed deliverable volume.

**W2 — Subscription definition, monthly Business and credits**  
https://intercom.help/waalaxy/en/articles/6413101-what-are-the-different-subscriptions  
Excerpt: “The standard price is €139/month, but it may vary depending on your country.”

One licence corresponds to one LinkedIn account. July 2026 help gives Business's standard monthly rate, Advanced's 25 monthly finder credits and Business's 500. Extra finder credits follow the main term; example €40/month for 1,000, charged €480 upfront on annual. No rollover or one-time credit purchase. Freemium retains data/CSV import but cannot automate. Trial language contradicts itself: one section says 7 days→monthly Advanced or 14→annual Advanced, another says monthly Business afterward. Say trial terms depend on the chosen offer; do not promise a specific automatic conversion.

**W3 — Team minimums, billing and discounts**  
https://intercom.help/waalaxy/en/articles/6414125-what-s-the-team-feature  
Excerpts: “10% discount from 5 accounts.” / “The discount code is not automatically applied”

Team members must share tier, term and renewal date. Seats correspond to LinkedIn accounts; base rate × seat count. Official example confirms Advanced annual €49/month = €588/year/seat excluding VAT, paid upfront. Inbox cannot be bought for a single account within the team. Team supports account access/permissions, pooled billing and cross-account duplicate checks. Discount: 5+ seats 10%; 10+ 15%; 20+ 20%; 30+ 25%; 40+ 30%. Request code from support and apply before paying. Scope of discount on Inbox is not explicit, so do not publish an exact discounted bundle total without confirmation.

For reference, applying 10% to five Business base seats alone gives €625.50/month; applying it to five Advanced annual base seats gives €2,646/year. Inbox remains an unresolved discount component. The main table therefore uses undiscounted bundles and discloses eligibility.

**W4 — Inbox prices**  
https://intercom.help/waalaxy/en/articles/6752157-what-is-the-waalaxy-inbox  
Excerpt: “Annually: €120 excluding tax”

Monthly €20; quarterly €48 total; annually €120 total. Includes conversation management, saved responses, tags and scheduled follow-ups. Combined with W3, budget it for every team seat when selected. Annual equivalent is €10/month, not €20.

**W5 — Cloud migration**  
https://intercom.help/waalaxy/en/articles/15587366-waalaxy-is-now-fully-cloud-based  
Excerpt: “Waalaxy no longer relies on a browser extension.”

July 1, 2026 article says campaigns run in the cloud and background regardless of browser activity. Supports any browser; optional Alien Copilot only simplifies imports. Do not repeat older claims that the Waalaxy Chrome extension/desktop must remain running.

**W6 — Replies**  
https://intercom.help/waalaxy/en/articles/5634090-how-does-a-campaign-work-conditions-delays-prospect-reply  
Excerpt: “they will be removed from this campaign”

Replies to connection notes, messages or email remove the prospect from the sequence and stop subsequent messages. Article also documents a maximum of 5,000 active prospects in a campaign; additional ones wait.

**W7 — Duplicate imports**  
https://intercom.help/waalaxy/en/articles/6424163-faq-waalaxy-imports  
Excerpt: “Waalaxy identifies them during import and skips them”

Existing prospects are skipped on import, with an option to move duplicates to the new list. Cross-teammate import suppression requires Team. Supports regular LinkedIn, Sales Navigator, Recruiter Lite and CSV sources.

**W8 — Sales Navigator and feature-gated integrations**  
https://intercom.help/waalaxy/en/articles/15869883-use-ai-with-waalaxy-copilot-skill-mcp-api  
Excerpt: “No, but it's strongly recommended.”

Answer is to whether Sales Navigator is needed. Ordinary operation does not require it. API/MCP requires Advanced or Business. This contradicts W2's broad claim that Pro has all Advanced features except quota; the narrower current integration documentation is safer.

**W9 — Term, cancellation and refund limits**  
https://intercom.help/waalaxy/en/articles/6413100-payment-cancellation-refund-of-subscriptions  
Excerpt: “up to 24 hours before the automatic renewal”

Monthly, quarterly and annual terms; quarterly/yearly are paid in one installment upfront. Cancel future renewal, retain access through current period. Help says to cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Its refund link points to a broken /terms-conditions URL.

https://www.waalaxy.com/terms-of-use  
Excerpt: “Any period started is due in full.”

Current terms §17 renew for the same duration and require payment through the current commitment. No general money-back promise was verified; the official FAQ says refund conditions depend on the chosen offer/checkout. Do not promise a refund window.

## Copy recommendations

- Lead with fit: Dripify for integrated LinkedIn/email with a small team; Expandi for conditional campaign control; HeyReach for several LinkedIn senders sharing one workflow and inbox; Waalaxy for template-led workflows where annual commitment and Inbox costs are acceptable.
- Use monthly list prices in the main table, with the Waalaxy Business + Inbox configuration named. If comparing LinkedIn-only Waalaxy Advanced, label its annual basis clearly instead.
- Keep a short pricing note: prices checked 8 November 2026; native currencies; taxes/LinkedIn/mailbox services excluded; five-seat Waalaxy code discount available.
- Useful feature distinctions: Dripify owner Advanced gate; Expandi Agency controls ambiguity; HeyReach workspace-scoped suppression and screen-level permissions; Waalaxy team-wide Inbox purchase and common billing term.
- Do not rank safety or results from marketing statements. No claim in these notes comes from our own product testing.
