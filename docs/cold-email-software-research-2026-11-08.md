## Instantly

- Canonical current outreach pricing now agrees with help: Growth $47/month; Hypergrowth $107/month; Light Speed $358/month. The earlier $97 Hypergrowth is superseded in current canonical page extraction.
- Displayed annual equivalents: Growth $37.60; Hypergrowth $85.58; Light Speed $286.30. The main text extractor labels both sets monthly, but official Instantly blog identifies the discounted group as billed annually. Do not present as cancellable monthly or round $85.58 up silently.
- Growth: 1,000 uploaded contacts, 5,000 email sends/month. Hypergrowth:25,000 uploaded contacts,125,000 sends. Stored-contact allowance does not reset monthly.
- Team invitations, agency view and replying from Unibox start at Hypergrowth. Do not use Growth in a shared-team cost example.
- Workspaces isolate clients; every workspace needs its own paid subscription. Three Hypergrowth client workspaces cost $321/month in software before domains/mailboxes/data.
- Mailboxes purchased through vendor: DFY Google $5/account/month; AirMail $4; prewarmed Google$10; domains$15/year. Include only when scenario actually buys these services.
- Data, CRM and inbox placement are separately billed subscription types.

# Cold email pricing: six vendor verification

Research date: 8 November 2026, America/Regina. USD. Monthly billing and annual equivalents labelled separately. Primary sources only. Research notes for implementation, not a public artifact.

## Eligible fixed workload examples

Scope: existing prospect list and existing paid mailboxes; subscription subtotal only, tax/domains/mailbox subscriptions/optional data excluded. Capacity is a comparison requirement, not a recommended sending rate.

| Tool | Solo: 1 user, 1 mailbox, 1,000 prospects, 3,000 monthly sends, 1 workspace | Team: 3 users, 3 mailboxes, SAME shared prospect/send volume, 1 workspace |
|---|---|---|
| Smartlead | Base $39/month | Pro $94/month; Base does not support inviting team members |
| lemlist | Email $69/month | Email $69/month; unlimited users/senders, 50,000 sends shared |
| Saleshandy | Starter $41/month | Pro $99/month; Starter one user, Pro ten |
| QuickMail | Starter $49/month | Starter $49/month; unlimited users/senders |
| Snov.io | Starter $39/month | Starter $39/month; unlimited team seats, 5,000 recipients |

Agency example: three separate client workspaces, each 1,000 prospects and 3,000 monthly sends (3,000 prospects/9,000 sends overall):
- Smartlead Pro $94 + three client workspaces x $29 = $181/month. Pro includes30,000 contacts/90,000 sends. Client-workspace add-ons available from Pro; Prime includes three, but costs more for this workload.
- lemlist three Email team subscriptions x $69 = $207/month. Each separate team has its own subscription and 50,000-send allowance.
- QuickMail Agency $299 includes two workspaces, plus one $49 workspace = $348/month; capacity100,000 contacts/500,000 sends.
- Do not assume Snov.io or Saleshandy's ordinary shared team equals three separately accessible client workspaces. Exact eligibility not verified.
- Omit Mailshake from fixed capacity scenarios unless its per-user/shared-send allowance is confirmed. Prices are per purchased user, but copy is inconsistent on address allowances.
